Inside the community

Two things to train.

Strength comes from the work you do and the habits that keep you doing it. The community covers both.

Body

The lifting

  • A plan matched to your goal, your schedule and your equipment.
  • Feedback on your technique, so the lifts you do are the lifts you intend.
  • Clear progression: when to add weight, when to hold, when to back off.

Brain

The thinking

  • The reason behind each exercise, explained in plain language.
  • Habits that survive busy weeks and low-motivation days.
  • A community that checks in on you and keeps you accountable.

How it works

How a call gets booked.

There's no calendar to wrestle with. You apply, I read it, and I email you to set up a time.

  1. Step 1

    Apply

    Answer a few questions about your goals and your training. It takes about two minutes.

  2. Step 2

    Talk

    I read your application and email you to arrange a call. We cover your goals, your training so far and what has been getting in the way.

  3. Step 3

    Decide

    If the community fits, I'll walk you through joining. If it doesn't, I'll tell you.

Is it for you?

Who this suits.

A good fit

  • You want a structured plan instead of guessing in the gym.
  • You're willing to log your training and take feedback.
  • You want to understand why you're doing what you're doing.

Probably not yet

  • You want a shortcut or a quick fix.
  • You can't commit to training regularly right now.
  • You'd rather not track your sessions or hear honest feedback.

FAQ

Before you apply.

What happens after I apply?

I read your application and reply by email to set up a call. If you don't see my message, check your spam folder.

Is the call a hard sell?

No. The call is for both of us to decide if the community is a fit. You're free to say no, and so am I.

How much does it cost?

I go over pricing on the call, once I know what you need. You can ask me anything before you decide.

Do I need to be experienced?

The application asks how long you've been lifting so I can come to the call prepared. Tell me where you are right now.
